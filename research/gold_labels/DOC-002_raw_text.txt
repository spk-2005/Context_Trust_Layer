# Guidelines For Engagement Of Employees On Contract Basis

No.G.11021/2/92-PAR(ARW) GOVERNMENT OF MIZORAM DEPARTMENT OF PERSONNEL & ADMINISTRATIVE REFORMS (ADMINISTRATIVE REFORMS WING) *** OFFICE MEMORANDUM Dated Aizawl, the 7th August, 2008 Subject :      Guidelines for Engagement of Employees on Contract basis.

In   partial   modification   of   this   Department's   O.M.   No.A.41020/1/2003- P&AR(GSW) dt.10.10.2004 and even number dt.04.04.2005 and dt.13.3.2008 the Governor of Mizoram is      pleased to prescribe the following terms and conditions for engagement of employees  on  contract  basis  to  be  effective  from  the  date  of  issue  of  this  Office Memorandum.

All    Administrative    Departments    and    Heads    of    Departments    should scrupulously  follow  the  following  terms  and  conditions  while  making  engagements  on contract basis :

## 1. No engagement on contract basis shall be made except with prior approval of

DP&AR  and  Finance  Department,  Government  of  Mizoram.  And  before submission  of  any  proposal  for  engagement  of  Contract  employee  by  any Department to DP&AR and Finance Department, prior approval of Minister of the Department concerned should invariably be obtained.

## 2. There should be a clear cut vacancy of posts for engagement of employees on

contract unless otherwise fully funded from CSS or by other outside Agency.

Provided  that  in  certain  special  circumstances,  contract  employment  may  be resorted  to  by  the  Department  with  prior  concurrence  of  the  DP&AR  and Finance  Department  and  with  final  approval  of  the  Chief  Minister  through Chief Secretary in the absence of specific sanctioned posts.

## 3. No formal appointment shall be issued while making engagement on contract

except after execution of Agreement in the prescribed form appended.

## 4. Period of engagement on contract basis shall be for one year at the initial stage.

Thereafter, they should be screened again by the duly constituted DPC to see whether they are fit for further retention or otherwise. If they are found fit for further retention, the appointing authority may extend their services for another period as it may deem fit and proper.

## 5. Retired  Officers  should  not  normally  be  engaged  on  contract  basis  beyond

attainment of 63 years of age unless there are overriding considerations in the public interest justifying the terms of engagement for a longer period.

## 6. No engagement on contract basis should be made against such post(s) having

financial  powers  and  a  contract  employee  should  neither  be  delegated  with financial  powers  nor  assigned  the  work  of  DDO  unless  there  are  overriding considerations in the public interest justifying such assignment.

## 7. Retired Officers re-employed on contract basis shall not necessarily be entitled

to the same facilities enjoyed while in active service.

## 8. The rate of monthly remuneration of contract employee shall be equal to the

minimum  scale  of  pay  attached  to  the  post  in  which  he/she  in  engaged  plus 50% of that amount.

## 9. All  Contract  employees  shall  be  entitled  to  Medical  Re-imbursement  at  par

with other regular government servants.

## 10. Contract employees in the Educational Institutions will also draw remuneration

for the whole year without any break during vacation.

## 11. Contract employees under  these  guidelines shall  imply  those  engaged  by  the

Government       of  Mizoram  except  those  re-employed  pensioners  on  Contract employment.

## 12. All   Contract   employments,   except   contract   re-employment   of   retired

Government  servants  shall  be  made  against  vacant  sanctioned  posts  which should  be  done  only  under  the  provisions              of  the  Employment  Exchanges (Compulsory  Notification  of  Vacancies)  Act,  1959  and  the  Rules  framed thereunder.

## 13. Contract employees shall be required to subscribe to special security scheme as

may be introduced by the Government of Mizoram from time to time.

## 14. Casual  Leave  shall  also  be  admissible  to  all  contract  employees  at  par  with

other Government employees in a calendar year.

## 15. Leave  entitlement  will  be  as  per  Appendix-II  to  CCS  (Leave)  Rules,  1972.

Further, maternity leave as per the provision of CCS (Leave) Rules, 1972 shall also be applicable to contract employees.

## 16. No household Peon shall be provided to any contract employee.
## 17. No residential telephone shall be provided to any contract employee.
## 18. In the event of death or discharge from service of a contract employee within

the contract period, gratuity at the rate of one-half of a months remuneration for  each  completed  year  of  engagement  shall  be  permissible  if  he  had completed not less than five years continuous engagement at the time of death or discharge from service. The emoluments for this purpose shall be restricted to  the  existing  remuneration  drawn  by  the  employee  at  the  time  of  death  or discharge from service.

## 19. In the event of death or discharge from service of a contract employee, 1/                         of

2 Earned leave at his credit subject to a maximum of 150 days shall be encashed.

The emoluments for this purpose shall be restricted to the existing remuneration drawn by the employee at the time of death or discharge from service.

## 20. Contract  employees  who  are  transferred  from  one  station  to  another  station

beyond 20 kms. will be entitled TA/DA at par with other regular employees.

Moreover,  TA/DA  on  official  tour  shall  also  be  admissible  to  all  Contract employees at par with other regular Government servants.

## 21. Service book shall be maintained for each contract employee.
## 22. In  respect  of  any  matter  in  which  no  provision  has  been  made  under  these

guidelines,   the   provisions   of   CCS(CCA)   Rules   1965,   any   orders   made thereunder and any other Rules made or deemed to be made under Article 309 of  the  Constitution  of  India  shall  apply  to  the  extent  to  which  they  are applicable   to   the   service   hereby   provided   for   and   the   decision   of   the Government as to their applicability shall be final.

## 2. The party of the first part shall be entitled to a remuneration at the rate Rs.________

_________________/- p.m.

## 3. The party of the first part shall devote his/her whole time to his duties and at a times

abide by the CCS (Conduct) Rules, 1964 and whenever required, proceed any part of India and there perform such duties as may be assigned to him/her.

## 4. The service of a contract employee will be terminated :
a)  automatically at the end of the contract period unless extended by the Government
b)  by the Government without previous notice if the Government is satisfied medical

evidence that the contract employee is unfit for further service.

c)  by giving 1 (one) month notice in writing at any time by either of the party except

as provided at (b) above

d)  by the  Government or their Officers having proper  authority without a previous

notice if the party of the first part is guilty of any insubordination intemperance or other misconduct or of any breach of any of the provision of these presents or of any rules pertaining to the office of the public service to which he may belong.
