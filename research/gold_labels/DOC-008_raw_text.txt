Employee Notice Period Policy Doc ID: WIN-HR-POL-007 | Version: 1.5 Policy Summary Our notice period policy ensures smooth transitions for all employees at Wingify Software Private Limited. Effective April 1, 2021, this policy outlines various aspects:

It defines the    duration of notice and payout options if notice cannot be served and applies to all current and future full-time employees. During the notice period, employees are expected to fulfill offboarding duties, return company property, and adhere to leave protocols.

Deductions from Full & Final Salary may include laptop and asset values, loan adjustments, taxes, WFH     allowance      recovery,    excess    leaves,    short   notice    periods,    joining   bonus     recovery,    and EPF/VPF deductions.

Notice   periods    vary   based    on  Wingify     levels  and   grades,    with   Grade    A&B   having a maximum duration of 90 days, Grade C having 60 days, and Grade D & E  having 30 days.

This   policy   aims   to  ensure    clarity,  consistency,    and   fairness   in  handling notice       periods   across different circumstances, facilitating smooth transitions for all employees.

## 1. Purpose

Our notice period policy is designed to ensure smooth transitions, whether you're moving on or experiencing     changes    within   the  organization.     It outlines   the expected duration        an employee must continue working after indicating their intention to leave, facilitating a proper handover of tasks  and   knowledge      transfer.   The   primary     goal  is  to  provide   clear   guidance    on  the   notice period's maximum duration and outline the payout options available if the notice period cannot be fully served. This approach promotes transparency and fairness in our employment practices, supporting effective planning and seamless transitions for both the departing employees and the company.

## 2. Applicability

The   policy   applies   to  all Permanent      Employees,      Interns   &   Contractors     of Wingify    Software Private Limited.

## 3. Policy

Our Notice Period Policy defines the requirements and expectations for employees serving their notice period in situations such as resignation, dismissal, redundancy, or retirement.

Effective    April    1,  2021,     this   policy    provides     clear    guidelines     to   ensure     consistency, professionalism, and smooth transitions in all cases involving employee departures.

### 3.1 Notice Periods Duration

Wingify   has   defined   a  notice   period   duration for    employees      as per   Wingify levels    and grades framework; refer to the table below:

| Level & Grade | Group Category | Maximum Duration Confirmed | Probationers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A (A1 & A2) | CXO | 90 Days | 15 days |
| B (B1 & B2) | SeniorManagement | 90 Days | 15 days |
| C (C1, C2 & C3) | Middle Management | 60 Days | 15 days |
| D (D1, D2 & D3) | Junior Management | 30 Days | 15 days |
| E (E1, E2 & E3) | Fellow | 30 Days | 15 days |
